What is Corporate Health Insurance?

A group health insurance policy acts as a safety net designed specifically for businesses to cover the medical expenses of their employees.

The Process

From Registration to Claims

A seamless, digitally-native experience for HR teams and employees alike.

1

HR Registration

Corporate HR teams register their company and provide basic organizational details to get started.

2

Policy Selection

HR selects from tailored insurance tiers. Our underwriters then review and approve the policy request.

3

Employee Onboarding

Once active, employees are added to the portal and can immediately access their insurance details.

4

Seamless Filing

Employees can file claims directly via their dashboard by uploading necessary medical documents.

5

Fast Settlement

Our claims officers review and process claims within 24-48 hours for swift reimbursement.
